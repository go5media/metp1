UK SAFETY CAMERA NETWORK

This page explains how the Metropolitan Police can request CCTV footage from the UK Safety Camera Network Ltd 

Requesting CCTV Footage

Metropolitan Police officers may request CCTV footage for legitimate policing purposes. All requests are subject to verification and review.

Access to Footage

CCTV footage is not automatically shared with police. Each request is assessed individually in accordance with applicable law.

Our Relationship

USCN operates independently of the Metropolitan Police Service. This page does not indicate any official partnership or affiliation.

Footage Availability

Footage availability depends on camera coverage, recording periods and applicable retention arrangements.
